Cosine similarity

cos(a, b) = a · b‖a‖ ‖b‖

Dot product: sum of the products of matching components. Length ‖a‖: square root of a · a. [Manning et al., §6.3.1]

The measures

When every vector has length 1

  • Dot product = cosine similarity. [Sentence-Transformers docs]
  • Euclidean distance² = 2 − 2 · cosine similarity.
  • All three give the same ranking, so use the cheapest. pgvector recommends inner product for normalised vectors. [pgvector README]
